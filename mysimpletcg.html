My Simple TCG! is a single-player trading card collecting game built around the everyday fun of opening packs, spotting value, making deals, and deciding what deserves a place in your binder. No pressure, no online account, just the loop card collectors know: buy, rip, sort, sell, regret, repeat.

Collect

Open packs, chase variants, organize binders, build sets, and hunt for the cards that make a collection feel personal.

Deal

Buy singles, haggle at the flea market, inspect binder lots, evaluate walk-in sellers, and learn when a cheap card is actually a steal.

Grow

Run your shop, list cards for sale, restore damaged finds, hire staff, work odd jobs, and unlock new ways to keep the card economy moving.
