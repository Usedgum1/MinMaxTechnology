My Simple TCG! is a single-player trading card collecting game built around the everyday fun of opening packs, finding value, making deals, and slowly turning a tiny card habit into a real shop.

Collect

Open packs, chase variants, sort your binder, and build sets across a growing card catalog.

Trade

Buy singles, haggle at the flea market, evaluate walk-in offers, and decide when a deal is worth the risk.

Build

Run your shop, list cards for sale, wash dishes for quick cash, restore damaged cards, and unlock new systems as you play.
