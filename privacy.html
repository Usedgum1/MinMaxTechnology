Data Not CollectedMy Simple TCG! does not collect personal information or user data.

Privacy at a Glance

We have an old-school philosophy about games: they should just be simple fun.

That's why My Simple TCG! doesn't require accounts, track your activity, or collect personal information. My Simple TCG! is designed as an offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data.

  • No account is required.
  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • No personal information is collected.

Game Data

Game progress, save data, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game's functionality and is not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

My Simple TCG! does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services that collect personal information from players.

Children's Privacy

Because My Simple TCG! does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the game's privacy practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.
